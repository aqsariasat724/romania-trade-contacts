Why the sample reads 100% and the run reads 41%

The 24 rows below were filtered to complete records so the formatting and data quality are easy to judge. They are not representative of yield. Most Romanian trade businesses publish a phone but no email, so crawling company websites roughly triples email coverage — from 17% to 53% here — but a 90% threshold is not reachable from public sources at this budget.

How the data is collected

Fully automated — Python, no manual entry at any stage

  1. Harvest

    Structured queries pull every business tagged under the five trades across Romania, checkpointed per query so a failed request never costs a completed one.

  2. Normalise and gate

    Phones are parsed to E.164. Only genuine Romanian numbers survive — nine national digits beginning 7, 2 or 3 — which also removes records that bleed in from across the border.

  3. Recover missing emails

    Where a record has a website but no address, the site and its contact page are fetched and parsed. Addresses are validated against a TLD list and common provider typos are repaired, so a misspelling printed on a live page is not passed through to you.

  4. Deduplicate

    Records collapse on phone, on email, and on company name with legal suffixes stripped, so two branches of one firm do not arrive as two rows.

Source and licence

Worth reading before the file is used commercially

  1. Base records — OpenStreetMap

    Company names, phone numbers and website addresses are derived from OpenStreetMap: © OpenStreetMap contributors, available under the Open Database Licence (ODbL) 1.0. A database derived from ODbL data carries attribution and share-alike obligations, so this notice needs to travel with the file.

  2. Email addresses — each company’s own site

    Emails do not come from the base records. Each one was read from the company’s own public website or contact page, so they sit outside the obligation above.

  3. Personal data removed

    Addresses belonging to a named individual are stripped and, where the company publishes one, replaced with its role address. Only role and company addresses ship, which keeps the file clear of GDPR personal data. Marketing contact still needs a lawful basis under Romanian Law 506/2004 — that sits with whoever sends the mail.
